Est. 2024 — Lagos, Nigeria

Founded on Knowledge

NOESIS Bank was born in 2024 in Lagos, Nigeria, from a simple belief: Nigerians deserve a bank that understands them. Not a foreign bank with a local branch — but a bank built here, for here.

We started with a team of 8 people, one office in Lekki, and a dream to make banking painless. Today, we're 50,000 customers strong with 12 branches across the country — and we're just getting started.

"We don't just hold your money. We help it grow."

Licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria and insured by NDIC, NOESIS combines the trust of traditional banking with the speed and convenience of fintech. Every feature we build starts with one question: Does this make life easier for our customers?
